We're thrilled to flip the switch on **Pulse Radio** — six always-on streams built for
every mood, from late-night lo-fi to heads-down techno.

## What you'll find

- **Chill** — slow beats and soft textures
- **House** — four-on-the-floor grooves
- **Rock** — indie and alt energy
- **Jazz** — warm late-night soul
- **Retro** — the 80s and 90s bangers
- **Techno** — relentless electronic pulses

Every station shows what's playing right now, a rolling song history, and a request
box so you can put your favourite track on the air.

> Good radio is a conversation. Hit request, say hi in the chat, and help shape the
> playlist.

Thanks for listening — turn it up.
